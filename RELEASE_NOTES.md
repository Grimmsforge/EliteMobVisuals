# Elite Mob Visuals release notes

Newest first. Each version's section is what goes in CurseForge's changelog
box when its file is uploaded, so it's written for players. Developer detail
lives in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-15)

### New

- **End pieces.** Two new styles that wrap the end of the health bar:
  **Clawed Clasp** and **Winged Gem**. Put one on the left end, the right
  end, or a mirrored pair on both. They take each classification's colour,
  like the other bundled styles.
- **Width slider** on every badge and marker, next to Size. Size sets the
  height; Width stretches or squashes the art to fit your plates. It works
  with every style, not only the end pieces.

### Tips

- Pick an end piece in the badge's **Style**, and set **Position** to *Left
  of plate* with **Mirrored pair on both sides** ticked to dress both ends.
- End pieces are taller than the bar they wrap. Start with Size at about
  three to four times your health bar's height for the clasp, and four to
  five times for the wings, then use **Width** and **Horizontal offset** to
  fine-tune.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

First release, for WoW Forever.

- A badge beside the nameplate of elite, rare, rare elite and boss mobs (your
  target's, or every one), and a marker above their heads.
- The same badge and marker on your target frame: always, or only when the
  nameplate can't show them.
- 13 styles, a colour and style per classification, size, position, glow,
  backgrounds with borders, and animations.
- Works with Blizzard, EllesmereUI, Plater, ElvUI and KuiNameplates
  nameplates, and Blizzard, EllesmereUI, ElvUI and Shadowed Unit Frames
  target frames.
- Options window with a live preview, test mode (`/emv test`), a page in the
  game's Settings, and a minimap button that minimap button bags can collect.
